## Highlights

- **New scaling for the HYTE Y70 and other Nexus panels.** Widget scale is now a slider in percent. Go below 100% to fit more, smaller widgets, or up to 200% on display panels with room for it. Each panel offers the steps that fit its screen, and existing layouts keep their size.
- **Remastered lighting animations.** Animation effects are brighter and more colorful, and their presets come in color moods such as warm, cool, neon, sunset or aurora instead of all being a rainbow. Bounce, Brush and Shine are redesigned and many more are redrawn.
- **An easier Lighting widget.** The Lighting widget shows a grid of color and animation swatches that fits its size. Tap one to switch your lighting, or the Open Lighting tile to go to Lighting.
- **Restricted mode for touch panels.** Lock a touch panel, such as a HYTE Y70's, to lighting, media and volume, so kids or guests can't change your widgets or layout. Turn it on from the panel's device page.

## Lighting

- Animation effects have new looks with fuller, more colorful defaults, and each effect's presets now take different color moods, such as warm, cool, neon, sunset or aurora, instead of all being a rainbow. Every effect takes its colors from the color wheel, so you can tune any preset's colors. Bounce, Brush and Shine are redesigned, and Mandala, Cosmic Dust, Sandstorm, Bubbles, Flow Field, Jellyfish, Lightning, Matrix, Neon Rain and Ribbon Flow are redrawn. Fire burns in red, orange and yellow layers, and Color Cycle runs faster and starts with an orange fade preset. Saved templates still load. Neon Rain no longer has a Splash control.
- Simple mode's animations now run effects from the Patterns category, which leads the effect list, so you can tune each one and pick its presets in advanced mode. Your saved lighting moves over.
- The brightness popup is now Brightness settings. Its new Brightness multiplier switch multiplies each device's brightness by master brightness instead of capping it, so devices keep their relative levels as you dim. It applies to the whole PC and starts off.
- A lighting device whose updates keep failing shows Not responding on the Devices page and a strip on its Lighting card. Click it, or Lighting doesn't match? at the end of the Lighting page's device list, to open Fix lighting: close conflicting apps, restart Nexus (it comes back to the Lighting page), then power the PC off completely.
- The onboard lights of the MSI MPG X870E CARBON WIFI show as their own zones on the Lighting page and take a solid color. Areas you don't assign keep the board's own effect.
- Clicking the selected effect again closes its editor.
- Frame cap offers 25 Hz.

## Panels

- The Lighting widget shows color and animation swatches sized to the widget, with a tile that opens Lighting. Show recently used, on by default, adds the colors and animations you last picked on the Lighting page.
- Restricted mode on a touch panel's device page limits the panel to lighting, media and volume. Other widgets and widget editing are locked, deck keys keep only lighting, media, volume and page switching, and the tray and control center show only a Restricted mode sign. It turns on by itself while Windows is in first-time setup, and a HYTE Y70 shows its Nexus panel during that setup.
- Widget scale is now a slider in percent. 100% is the standard grid and lower values fit more, smaller widgets. A HYTE Y70 offers 100, 75 and 50%, display panels also 150 and 200% where they fit, and cooler and device screens 100 and 50%, with 75% on the Lian Li Universal Screen 8.8. The Aftershock Glacier Matrix LCD gets Widget scale too: 100% keeps its single wide tile and 50% lays it out as a grid of widgets. Existing layouts keep their size. On touch panels, widgets that no longer fit move to the next pages; on other displays, Nexus lists the widgets that won't fit before removing them.
- Streaming panels to device screens is faster on Windows. Frames reach the screen with less copying and on the CPU's performance cores, and a page no longer loses a few frames per second below the rate it asks for.
- Frame cap is now set for each streamed display on its device page, such as a pump or fan screen, and replaces the single Streamed display frame cap in panel settings. Every display starts with no cap.
- Use as a secondary monitor on the Lian Li Universal Screen 8.8, the Lian Li HydroShift II OLED Curved and the Aftershock Glacier Matrix LCD now shows the device's own Nexus panel on that monitor, and comes back on after Nexus restarts. Turn off the Nexus panel to see the Windows desktop there instead. Only one device can use it at a time, and the page names the device that has it. Windows only.
- On a panel's device page, hover the preview's background and click Edit background for Background options, Import media and GIFs. On a display that shows a single widget, the preview offers Edit widget and Edit background, and the widget can be removed. The Blank widget is gone. At 0% Background Opacity, a note under the slider says the background isn't rendering.
- The widget menu starts with the widget's name. Click it to open the widget in immersive mode or open its page, which replaces the separate Immersive mode item.
- The OLED screen on the SteelSeries Apex Pro TKL (2023) and Apex Pro TKL Gen 3 shows a Nexus panel in black and white, on Windows and Linux. The screen goes back to the keyboard while your PC is locked. Turn on Nexus Control for it on the Devices page to start. Thanks spudge!

## Devices

- ASUS ROG Astral GeForce graphics cards get a device page on Windows. Level Sense shows how far the card sags and tilts, on cards with a tilt sensor, with Calibrate to set what level is. Power detector shows the current on each pin of the 12V-2x6 connector to two decimals, can notify you when a pin nears or passes its limit, and can shut the PC down when a pin stays over its limit for a set time (Overcurrent shutdown, which you can cancel) or right away at a critical level (Critical overcurrent shutdown). Card uses a ROG Equalizer cable raises the limits. Thanks @GrPK!

## Cooling

- You can keep up to 20 custom fan curves, up from 10. Presets count toward the limit.

## Windows

- When Windows Dynamic Lighting is on and a compatible device is connected, Nexus lists it with conflicting apps as a Windows setting with a Turn off button, since it fights Nexus for the same lights. Turn on Use Windows Dynamic Lighting under Conflicting apps in Settings and Nexus leaves it alone.

## Linux

- On Hyprland, Nexus places the HYTE Y70 panel on the Y70 and maps its touch input to it. On any desktop, the panel also opens when the Y70 display shows up after Nexus has started.
- The Corsair Elite Capellix LCD, Corsair XC7 RGB Elite LCD and ID-Cooling FX-LCD show Nexus panels on Linux. Turn on Nexus Control for them on the Devices page to start.

## Also in this release

- The Build widget is in the widget picker. On Windows and Linux it shows the picture of your PC from the Build page, and a PC in a HYTE Y70 case shows a picture of the case until then.
- On the dashboard's home page, hovering a widget shows a More actions button on its corner.
- The search box in the top bar grows with a wider window.
- Most monitoring gauge styles show their reading larger.

## Fixes

- On some Windows PCs, the desktop, games or a drawing tablet's pen stuttered every few seconds while Monitoring was on.
- On some MSI motherboards, ARGB header lighting changed in visible steps instead of smoothly and lagged behind.
- On some MSI motherboards, such as the MPG X870E CARBON WIFI, the board's own lights switched off when Nexus started.
- Smart lights, such as Govee, Hue and Nanoleaf, could drop out of and back into Lighting every few seconds, or blink to black, when they missed a single status check.
- Diagnostics flagged some Seagate drives to watch with billions of command timeouts.
- Applying a lighting scenario from an AI agent through AI Integration replaced your saved settings for that effect with its defaults.
- A 2023 NZXT Kraken's display stayed on NZXT's own screen, and the cooler's fans could stop following your fan curves.
- A Corsair Elite Capellix LCD showing a page that doesn't change went back to its own picture after a few seconds.
- On a Tryx Panorama, the network item in the display's overlay showed -- instead of the network speed.
- A touch display used as a display panel, such as a touch bar, showed only one page and no longer opened immersive mode on a tap.
- On a touch panel such as a HYTE Y70, a Web View widget disappeared or redrew while you swiped between pages.
- Updating from an older version could move desktop widgets that sat on a HYTE Y70 to your main display.
- Opening the weather page from a Weather widget set to its own location showed a different place.
- On panels, monitoring gauge labels that wrap onto two lines were not centered.
- On a panel's device page, the playlist arrows shrank the preview.
- On touchscreen PCs, dropdown lists had no search box.
- On Linux, a HYTE Y70's Brightness tab could stay missing until Nexus restarted.
- On Linux with a compositor such as Hyprland, the HYTE Y70 panel could fail to open until it was switched off and on.
- On Linux, stopping or restarting Nexus could hang while a panel was open.
