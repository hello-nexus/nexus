## Highlights

- **Lian Li HydroShift II OLED Curved.** Its curved OLED becomes a Nexus panel you can fill with widgets, the pump and coolant temperature join the Cooling page, and the 35 edge LEDs play your Lighting page effects. The panel's Settings tab also tilts and raises the screen.
- **Stream Deck dials and touch screens.** The Elgato Stream Deck +, Stream Deck + XL, Stream Deck Studio and Corsair Galleon K100 SD now work with Nexus, dials and screens included, and the Stream Deck Neo gets its info screen and touch keys. Set each dial to volume, app volume, microphone volume, brightness, a Monitoring readout or page turning, or give it your own actions for turns, presses and touches.
- **Radeon fan control.** Nexus now drives the fans of AMD Radeon RX 5000 series and newer graphics cards on Windows, which ignored your curves before. When Nexus lets go of the fan, it puts back the card's own fan curve and Zero RPM setting.
- **Lian Li Universal Screen 8.8.** The 8.8" screen works as a full Nexus widget panel, in landscape or standing on its short side with Portrait mount under Mounting on its Settings tab, and its 60-LED frame is laid out around the screen on the Lighting page.
- **Widget scale.** More screen real estate: the HYTE Q60 and Q80, the Corsair iCUE LINK 5" LCD, the HydroShift II OLED Curved and the Lian Li Universal Screen 8.8 get a Widget scale setting on their Widgets tab. Large shows fewer, bigger widgets and Small fits more on a denser grid. On a Q-series cooler, Small turns the one-widget screen into a grid of widgets, and Nexus lists any widgets that will not fit before it changes the grid.
- **Font choices for panels.** Pick a font for each panel under Theme > Text > Font on its page in Nexus: Lexend, Oswald, Tektur, Jura or Piazzolla. Every widget on that panel draws in it.

## Lian Li

- The HydroShift II OLED Curved can play a video you upload instead of the Nexus panel, and a Screen saver plays a video every few minutes to protect the OLED from burn-in. Pump follows motherboard lets the pump follow its motherboard header when Nexus is not controlling it, and when Nexus lets go the cooler returns to its own screen.
- The HydroShift II LCD-C now works over USB like the LCD-S: its round screen becomes a Nexus panel, its pump, fans and coolant temperature join the Cooling page, and its pump ring plays your Lighting page effects.
- The Galahad II LCD's pump and coolant temperature join the Cooling page, with the pump's full speed range that the motherboard pump header could not reach. Its pump-head ring lights up as a single-color zone on the Lighting page instead of staying dark.

## Corsair

- The iCUE LINK TITAN II pump and the iCUE LINK 5" LCD module show by name on an iCUE LINK System Hub, with their lighting, speed and temperature. The 5" LCD's screen becomes a Nexus panel, marked Experimental and named Corsair TITAN II LCD when it sits on a TITAN II.
- Nexus pauses an iCUE LINK System Hub whenever iCUE is running, stops writing the moment another program talks to the hub, and waits for a quiet minute before taking it back. Writing alongside iCUE could break iCUE's saved profile on the hub and leave its fans dark.

## Stream Deck

- On a deck with dials, a Keys and Dials switch above the action list on the Customize tab shows the dial actions, and the preview takes the shape of your deck. A dial can stack several actions that a press cycles through, and holding an empty dial, or long-touching an empty spot on the touch strip, opens it for editing in Nexus.
- The Stream Deck Neo's info screen shows the clock or the page number, set with Info Screen on the Settings tab, and its touch keys turn pages.
- Presets live in the preset menu at the top of the device page, next to Recent Apps. Trigger with apps... switches a deck to a preset while one of its apps is in focus, replacing the App Aware mode, and the per-app starter presets open from Import starter presets... in the same menu.
- Importing an Elgato profile brings its dial actions along, and an Open action with a Windows command such as calc now launches it. Keys can also take a Screenshot or Screen recording action, which opens your system's capture tool.

## Panels

- The rendering engine that draws cooler screens, panels and Stream Deck keys got a little faster.
- Big screens Nexus draws on the PC, such as the HydroShift II OLED Curved and the Universal Screen 8.8, render at a lower resolution by default to use less CPU while keeping small text readable. Set Render mode to High resolution under Rendering on the panel's Settings tab for full detail.
- On a panel without touch, such as the Universal Screen 8.8, you can now drag, resize and remove widgets in the preview on its page in Nexus, as on a touch panel.
- Cooler screens Nexus draws on the PC start on a solid background instead of an animated one, which saves CPU, unless you picked a background yourself. HYTE Q-series coolers keep their animated default.
- Page dots on touch panels are larger and stay at the bottom of the screen in landscape.
- The Diagnostics widget is offered only on touch panels and the dashboard, since it works by tapping.

## Cooling

- While AMD Software's automatic GPU tuning is on, the Radeon fan's card on the Cooling page shows a warning triangle explaining how to set GPU tuning to Custom, and the fan follows Nexus again once you do.
- When Nexus hands your fans to the BIOS because fan control kept stalling, it now takes them back after 5 minutes without a stall. If it happens a second time, the fans stay with the BIOS until you restart Nexus or switch CPU thermal guard off and on in Settings > Lighting & Cooling.
- A USB cooler that stops answering, such as a HydroShift II, no longer holds up control of your other fans or trips the thermal guard. It shows as disconnected until it answers again, then picks its curves back up.

## Lighting

- Sony DualShock 4, DualSense and DualSense Edge controllers and the GameSir Nova 2 Lite are no longer taken over for lighting, which could take the controller away from games and Steam Input. They show as switched-off cards on the Lighting page; switch one on to light it.
- A search button on the device list of the Lighting page and the fan list of the Cooling page filters the list by name, hub or group.

## Widgets

- A 2x2 Monitoring widget, square or round, can show two sensors, 2 side by side or 2 stacked, picked in its settings. The small readings of the 1 large + 2 small layout offer more designs.
- AIO pump speeds appear in the Cooler category of widget sensor pickers next to the coolant temperature, so a cooler screen can show both.

## Updates

- With Update channel set to Beta in Settings > General, Nexus updates only to newer betas, so a beta install is never moved onto a stable release by an update.

## Windows

- Apps, files, folders and links opened from a deck key come to the front instead of opening behind the window you are in, Task Manager included.
- If antivirus quarantines Nexus during setup, the installer says so, explains how to restore the file and add an exception for the Nexus folder, and lets you try again instead of showing a bare file error.

## Linux

- The installer warns when fontconfig is missing, which Stream Deck keys and LCD screens need to draw, and names the package to install.

## Also in this release

- Turning on Nexus Control for a device marked Experimental now asks you to confirm each time, and the conflicting-apps popup and onboarding mark experimental devices with the Experimental badge.
- Device names carry their brand on the Devices page, device pages and top bar, such as Elgato Stream Deck + and Corsair Xeneon Edge. The sidebar shows the brand's logo instead and sorts devices by brand, and HYTE devices show the HYTE logo.
- Nollie controllers are no longer marked Experimental.

## Fixes

- Turning Nexus Control on or off for Memory no longer crashes Nexus.
- When an NVIDIA driver update restarts Nexus, Diagnostics lists it as a Driver update restart on the GPU timeline instead of an app crash.
- A song started from search in a browser or the Spotify app now shows its album art without skipping away and back.
- The Lighting widget no longer counts too few lit devices after Nexus starts.
- A touch panel opens on its first page after Nexus restarts instead of the last page it showed, and the blank page after the last one now shows on both the panel and its page in Nexus.
- A widget's menu opened near the right edge of a panel stays next to where you pressed instead of jumping away.
- On a round cooler screen's page in Nexus, the widget settings preview now matches the size and crop on the screen.
- On a 4x4 Media widget on a touch panel, a two-line title no longer pushes the album art and seek bar out of place.
- Importing Elgato profiles no longer stops at ten Stream Deck presets.
- The pump ring of a wireless HydroShift II LCD-C now starts at the top of the head, so effects line up with it.
- A cooler screen connected over USB, such as a HydroShift II, comes back as a Nexus panel after it is unplugged and plugged in again.
- On macOS, a running Elgato Stream Deck app now shows as a conflicting app.
