Nexus 3.1.0 is the first release on the Production channel.

## Production and Beta

- Production gets releases that have been through a beta first. Beta gets new features and experimental device support first, and may be less stable.
- Change channel under Settings > General > Update channel. Nexus shows the version it will install, then downloads and installs it right away, in either direction. After that, each install updates only within its channel.
- Lian Li and Corsair devices are marked Experimental, and Nexus Control for them is available on Beta only. On Production their page says so and offers Switch to Beta.

## Highlights

- **Screens sleep while your PC is locked.** NZXT Kraken and Tryx Panorama screens now turn off when you lock your PC and fade back in when you unlock it. Each has a Turn off display when PC is locked switch on its page, on by default.
- **Dim lights when idle.** Settings > Lighting & Cooling > Brightness schedule has a new Dim lights when idle option: pick how far to dim and whether it starts after a set time or when your screen turns off. It works whether or not the schedule is on.
- **Monitor panels sized to the monitor.** A monitor turned into a panel from Devices > Displays now gets a grid sized from the monitor's real size, about one row per inch: a 24" monitor gets 12 rows, a 32" 4K monitor 16. Its Widget scale setting switches to Large for half as many rows and twice the widget size. Monitors turned into panels before this release move to the new grid, so their widgets may need rearranging.

## Panels

- The ASUS ROG Ryujin III's screen shows a Nexus panel, fitted to its landscape glass, and the ASUS logo marks it in the sidebar.

## Cooling

- The small fan on the Tryx Panorama's pump head joins the Cooling page as VRM Fan, and the motherboard header the pump runs on is labeled as the pump. When Nexus lets go, the fan returns to the cooler's own setting.

## Lighting

- The MSI MEG X870E GODLIKE's Dragon, MEG, Enthusiast and GODLIKE logos light up with your effects, and the MSI MPG X870E Edge Ti's IO shield joins the Lighting page.
- The AORUS GeForce RTX 5080 MASTER ICE lights its three fans, backplate and side logo as separate zones.

## Widgets

- The Media widget's settings have a Shown app option that keeps one app on screen, playing or paused, and its volume slider follows that app.

## Stream Deck

- A Stream Deck or Corsair Galleon K100 SD that Nexus has never controlled shows on the Devices page with its Nexus Control switch, so you can turn it on.

## Windows

- On Windows 10, the screens Nexus draws on the PC now show the panel instead of staying blank.

## Linux

- On KDE Plasma with Brave or Chrome installed from Flathub, the Y70 shows the same layout as its editor, and changes made on the panel survive a reboot.

## Also in this release

- ASUS, ASRock, Thermalright and ID-Cooling cooler screens are no longer marked Experimental.

## Fixes

- Changing a fan speed or curve while a cooling preset was loaded could freeze Nexus until it was restarted.
- The Mirror lighting effect no longer freezes on its last frame after a fullscreen game starts or the display mode changes.
- Monitor brightness changes made after a pause are no longer dropped, and a short brightness drag on a Y70 Touch no longer leaves brightness unresponsive until Nexus restarts.
- App icons on Deck widgets that stayed blank after a restart now load as soon as Windows is ready.
- The top bar shows a cooler screen's name on its page instead of Devices.
