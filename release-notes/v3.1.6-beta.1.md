## Highlights

- **Global hotkeys.** A new Settings > Hotkeys tab holds keyboard shortcuts that work from any app, even with the Nexus window closed. Show or hide Nexus, turn lighting on or off, and step brightness up or down start out on Ctrl+Alt+N, L, = and - (Cmd+Option on macOS), and any deck key or dial can get a hotkey of its own. Available on Windows and macOS.
- **Corsair Commander Core fans and pump on Windows.** The fans, pump and temperature probes on a Corsair Commander Core now show on the Cooling page and follow your fan curves, while Nexus keeps driving its lighting.
- **New cooler and motherboard screens.** The ASUS ROG Strix SLC IV LCD and the Corsair Nautilus LCD Cap can show a Nexus panel, and the ROG Crosshair X870E Glacial LCD gets its own device page. Turn on Nexus Control for them on the Devices page to start.
- **NZXT Kraken and LCD screens on Linux.** NZXT Kraken coolers now connect on Linux, and a Kraken's display, the Lian Li Universal Screen 8.8 and Lian Li wireless LCD fans can show Nexus panels there.

## Hotkeys

- Nexus actions on the Hotkeys tab lists the built-in hotkeys and any you add with Add hotkey. The x inside a field clears its combo, and delete on the right removes the hotkey. Reset defaults puts the built-in combos back and leaves your own hotkeys alone. A hotkey needs Ctrl, Alt or Cmd/Win unless it is an F key, and one that another app already uses shows as Unavailable.
- A deck key or dial has a Global hotkey field in its editor. Those hotkeys are listed under Deck keys on the Hotkeys tab, with Open in deck editor next to each.
- Deck keys can also turn lighting on or off and step its brightness, and a Stream Deck key can show or hide Nexus.

## Lighting

- Beat Builder has a new Stereo split switch. With it on, the left half of the effect follows the left audio channel and the right half the right channel. It starts off, so both halves follow the same sound as before. Beat Builder's other on/off options are now switches too.
- Hold Shift while resizing to keep the aspect ratio, for a device on the Lighting page, selected LEDs in Customize device, or a panel widget dragged by its corners.
- MSI ARGB Gen2 fans on the JRAINBOW3 header of supported MSI motherboards now light up and follow your lighting, one color per fan.

## Cooling

- Corsair Commander Core fans and pump get speed control and fan curves on Windows, and its coolant and other temperature probes show up as sensors. The pump never runs below 50%, and when Nexus stops controlling the Commander Core, its fans and pump go back to safe fixed speeds.
- The NZXT RGB & Fan Controller shows its fan speeds in Cooling and runs its fans on your fan curves, with empty fan headers hidden. Turn on Nexus Control for it on the Devices page to start; its lighting is unchanged.

## Panels

- The ASUS ROG Strix SLC IV LCD shows a Nexus panel on its display.
- The Corsair Nautilus LCD Cap shows a Nexus panel on its round display, with no need for iCUE.
- The ROG Crosshair X870E Glacial LCD has a device page for the modes the display runs on its own: Monitor, with up to three widgets that can each show any sensor under a label you choose, one of its built-in pictures, or an animation. Brightness, Display on and Stay on while asleep are on the same page.
- Monitoring widgets that show their sensors as rows have a new Label + Value design, which shows each sensor as plain text with no bar, and an Align setting for left, center or right.
- On a panel's device page, Media background tiles follow the shape of the panel's display. On a round display, such as an NZXT Kraken's, the tiles are round and the picture cropper shows a circle guide.
- A Media background that is empty or fails to load shows the panel's solid background color, and Nexus keeps retrying it.
- When Nexus can't save a change to a panel, it now says so.

## Lian Li

- LCD Flex fans on an L-Wireless Controller show on the controller's Screens tab, which has a themes section for each fan type. Built-in themes turns on the fan screen's own themes, with live readings sent over the radio: pick the Data source, such as CPU temperature or RAM used, and the graph colors. Turn it off to leave the screen to USB content. Unbinding a fan turns its built-in theme off, so its screen does not come back showing the theme.
- LCD Flex fans driven over the radio now have the same lighting zones as over USB, Center, Inner and Outer on SL-INF, Up and Down on TL with Swap up and down. Your saved zone settings move over once.
- A Flex fan plugged in by USB and still bound to the L-Wireless Controller reads Driven by this PC's radio on its page, with Open L-Wireless page.
- When you have several Flex screens of one kind, they are numbered from 1, and the Cooling page, the Screens tab and the Lighting page use the same names.

## Linux

- NZXT Kraken coolers connect on Linux with pump, fan and lighting control, and the same Kraken no longer shows up a second time from liquidctl or the kernel's own driver.
- A Kraken's display, the Lian Li Universal Screen 8.8 and Lian Li wireless LCD fans show Nexus panels on Linux. These screens need a Chromium-family browser such as Chrome, Chromium, Brave, Edge or Vivaldi, installed as a native package; a Flatpak browser cannot draw them. Thanks spudge!
- Nexus no longer controls AMD graphics card lighting on Linux, which could hang the card. NVIDIA-based cards keep their lighting.
- On Linux, while Lighting is on, Nexus no longer reads or controls a Corsair Commander Core, Core XT or Commander ST through liquidctl, which made its lighting blink and ignore changes. Its fans, pump and coolant temperature do not show in Cooling or follow fan curves while Lighting is on.

## Also in this release

- Monitoring's Detailed tab has a search box. It filters sensors by name, device or group as you type.
- Settings now also appears in the sidebar under Show more, and its tabs get scroll arrows when they don't fit.
- The Gallery holds up to 5,000 items across all its folders and files, up from 500 per folder, and its page shows them 100 at a time. Past that, a note says only the first 5,000 are shown.
- On the Devices page, each LED counts slider sits on one row with its header, and a header with no count yet shows 0.

## Fixes

- A Roccat Vulcan II Max set to a non-US layout, such as German, turned off lighting on every device. The keyboard now lights up, and a device that fails like this no longer stops the others.
- On Linux, Nexus could hang an AMD Radeon RX 9000 series graphics card a few seconds after starting, freezing the display until a reboot.
- With a Corsair iCUE LINK hub, fans stayed lit when the PC was locked, at 0% brightness or on solid black.
- On some MSI motherboards, such as the MAG Z790 Tomahawk WiFi, the JRAINBOW3 header stayed dark.
- On Windows, after you touched a Web View on a touch display such as a HYTE Y70 Touch, the mouse pointer stayed hidden over Nexus until Nexus restarted.
- A HYTE Y70 that Windows runs at a different resolution, such as 1080x1920, could show a phone layout with only a few widgets instead of its own panel.
- Screen Mirror of a display rotated to portrait came out black, and a landscape (flipped) display came out upside down.
- Some Lian Li LCD Flex fan screens were never detected, for example on fans connected by USB and also bound to an L-Wireless Controller.
- An onboarding hint could stay at its old spot after the sidebar collapsed.
