## Highlights

- **Web View widget.** Show any website live on your Y70 or a touch display turned into a panel on your Windows PC. Tap the tile to open the site fullscreen and use it with your fingers, with Back, Reload and Close at hand. Each widget has its own Zoom (0.25x to 3x) and Auto-refresh (every 1, 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes), and you set the address from the panel editor on your PC. Import from Nexus 2 brings your iFrame widgets over as Web View widgets.
- **Decks that are ready to use.** New Deck widgets start with an Essentials set of keys (volume down, volume up, screenshot, Task manager), and Media and System presets sit next to it. A deck can now be a 4x1 strip along one row, and Launch app takes any path or link you paste.
- **Always on top for the Y70.** A new Always on top switch under Show Panel on the Y70's page lets other windows appear over the panel, so a window you drag onto it stays in view (with Keep panel clear of other windows off). It is on by default.
- **Turned-off features leave your hardware alone.** With Lighting or Cooling turned off, Nexus no longer takes over your hubs and coolers, and on a new install nothing is taken over until you finish the How much control do you want? step.

## Panels

- The Y70's Keep the mouse off the panel switch now sits right under Keep panel clear of other windows.
- The Immersive background option shows only on touch screens, where immersive views exist.
- Lighting and Cooling on a touch panel remember which devices you selected, per panel, across restarts.

## Deck

- New Deck widgets get the Essentials keys, and Media (previous, play/pause, next, mute) and System (lock, sleep, screen recording, Settings) presets are added next to it, on existing installs too. Your existing decks are not changed. The presets show in your language until you rename them.
- Deck widgets can now use Screenshot and Screen recording keys, which used to be offered only on physical decks such as Stream Deck, plus a new Task manager key. On Windows, Screenshot and Screen recording open the Snipping Tool overlay, and Screen recording has a camcorder icon.
- The Deck widget has a 4x1 size that snaps to single rows.
- Launch app accepts a typed drive path or an http, https or steam:// link, with a Browse button beside the search box and a hint under it when the app is not in the list.

## Nexus 2 import

- The Y70 dock comes over as a 4x1 deck along the bottom of your first panel page, in a preset named Nexus 2 dock: app shortcuts launch the app, files and folders open, websites open their address, one-key recordings become hotkeys, and sound clips play. Importing again updates that preset instead of adding a second deck.
- iFrame widgets become 4x4 Web View widgets showing the same site at the same zoom. A widget with no usable address, or with no room on its page, is skipped with a note.
- The first import turns off Always on top and Keep panel clear of other windows on the Y70, so the panel behaves as it did in Nexus 2. Importing again keeps your choices.

## Widgets

- Weather (4x4): each day's range bar is colored by temperature, with a dot marking today's current temperature. Turn on Accent color bars for bars in your accent color instead.
- Volume mixer: drag devices into the order you want under Output order and Input order in the widget settings, and Nexus remembers it. New Show labels and Show output menu switches, and Hide idle apps becomes Show idle apps.

## Settings

- Settings for a feature you have turned off (Lighting, Monitoring events, the thermal guard, drive polling) are replaced by a single note saying which switch to turn on, and search lands on that note. The four feature switches carry a Master switch label.
- With Cooling off, onboarding no longer offers to import fan curves from FanControl or Nexus 2.
- The competing-app alert counts an app only when it competes for a feature you have turned on, and for a screen only while Nexus is driving that screen.
- Drive health (SMART) reads stop while Monitoring and Diagnostics are both off and no fan curve follows a drive.

## Lighting

- On a PC with an Intel integrated GPU and a dedicated graphics card, the Auto render GPU now uses the dedicated card. Rendering on the Intel GPU kept about one CPU core busy in the background.
- Kingston Fury memory updates its lighting faster.
- Newly supported: Corsair K70 RGB PRO Optical, Corsair M75 Wireless on its Slipstream receiver, Corsair Scimitar Elite RGB, Roccat Vulcan II Mini, SteelSeries Rival 3 Wireless, NZXT F280 RGB Core, Razer mice on the Mouse Dock Pro, and the Gigabyte GeForce RTX 4070 Ti SUPER Eagle OC ICE, RTX 4070 Ti SUPER AORUS MASTER, RTX 5060 Ti Aero OC 16G, Radeon RX 9060 XT GAMING ICE, RX 9070 GRE GAMING OC and RX 6750 XT GAMING OC 12G, MSI GeForce RTX 5060 Ti 16G and RTX 5060 8G GAMING TRIO OC, ASRock Radeon RX 7900 XTX Taichi 24GB OC and Colorful iGame RTX 5080 Ultra W OC.

## Lian Li

- Lian Li wireless fans and HydroShift II rings stay smooth when devices are pinned.
- A wireless fan chain that turns up paired to any other dongle pairs back to yours on its own, unless you unbound it yourself.

## Windows

- When antivirus blocks Setup, its dialog now has a Learn more button that opens the antivirus help page.

## macOS

- Launch app keys accept any app or file path on a Mac, and Browse picks one.

## Fixes

- Screen Mirror lighting could stay dark when Nexus started with Windows, or after its helper restarted.
- The Lighting page forgot which devices you had selected.
- The static color grid could pick the swatch in the row next to the one you clicked or tapped, and a second finger or palm could steer the pick.
- Lighting could freeze on a Corsair Lighting Node or Hydro Platinum cooler that stopped answering.
- The same memory module could be detected twice.
