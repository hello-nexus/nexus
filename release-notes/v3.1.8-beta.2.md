## Highlights

- **See your case's airflow in 3D.** The Cooling page's new Airflow view shows where air flows through your case, where heat builds up, and whether you have positive or negative pressure. Flip a fan between intake and exhaust and see the difference instantly. HYTE Y70, Y50 and X50 for now.
- **Your specs on the dashboard.** The Build widget's new 4x2 size shows your CPU, graphics card and memory next to your PC, and Build has its own spot in the sidebar.
- **Fans never stop if fan control stalls.** They speed up instead, and one click brings your curves back.
- **Lian Li wireless fans stay bound**, even after a full power off.

## Lighting

- Unbound Lian Li wireless fans and Strimers now show up on the Lighting page, ready to bind.
- New: ASUS ROG Strix Scope NX TKL Moonlight White, ASUS ROG Zephyrus G16 keyboard, Razer Basilisk V4 Pro, MSI Clutch GM11, Kreo Swarm (wired), TUXEDO Stellaris 16 Gen7 AMD keyboard, Framework Desktop, MSI PRO B850-S EVO WIFI and Z890 GAMING PLUS WIFI6E, and graphics cards from Gainward, MSI, Colorful and ASRock. Several Palit RTX 30 cards work again.
- Better lighting on ASRock graphics cards, Cooler Master ARGB Gen2, Akko PC75B Plus and Mountain Everest 60.

## Fixes

- Your fan preset keeps all your fans after an update or restart.
- Lian Li wireless fans keep spinning when you close Nexus.
- The CPU overheat guard now speeds up every fan, including ones left on motherboard control.
- Patterns effects such as Rainbow, Brush and Shine match the preview again.
- Panel text in Adaptive color no longer flickers.
- HYTE Y70: Always on top is back, and the panel appears during Windows setup.
- Frosted glass on desktop panels looks right again, including after changing widget padding.
- A dashboard page that failed to load once no longer stays broken, and browser page translation no longer crashes the dashboard.
- Linux: your motherboard's fan settings come back when you close Nexus, fan presets leave graphics card fans alone, and opening Nexus twice works.
