## Highlights

- **Effect Studio.** Build your own animated lighting effect by stacking blocks such as wipes, scanners, chases, flames, spectrum bars, VU meters and beat rings, or start from a starter or Surprise me. Any knob can move with the music or with a wave at a set rate, and Show on all devices plays the effect on your hardware while you edit. Saved effects run like the built-in ones. Open it with the Effect Studio button beside the effect search on the Lighting page's Animation tab.
- **A different look on every device.** Each device can now run its own lighting: different animations on different devices, or a mix of static colors, animations, media and screen mirroring, each assigned on its own. On the Lighting page, select the cards and pick what they should show; every other device keeps its look.
- **Web View widget.** Show any website live on your Y70 or a touch display turned into a panel on your Windows PC. Tap the tile to open the site fullscreen and use it with your fingers, with Back, Reload and Close at hand. Each widget has its own Zoom (0.25x to 3x) and Auto-refresh (every 1, 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes), and you set the address from the panel editor on your PC.
- **Control center on touch panels.** Swipe down from the top edge of a Y70 or a touch monitor panel for brightness, volume with per-app volumes, mic, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Airplane mode, dark mode, media controls, Lock PC, Sleep PC and Screen sleep.

## Lighting

- A search box above the effects finds any effect by name, and your Effect Studio effects lead the list under Custom.
- The new Clock fill effect sweeps around like a clock hand, with one to six hands, and Dot Matrix becomes an LED scoreboard with comets racing along its rows.
- Hovering a mode tab, a color or an effect outlines the cards your pick will change, the chip above the effects says what it applies to ("Applies to 2 selected"), and the cards that change pulse once.
- In Simple mode, devices with their own look bring up Control all, which puts every device back on one look.
- Each LED takes its color from a small spot right under it, and the strips on Lighting page cards show the colors your LEDs are really showing.
- The Lighting page remembers which devices you selected, and Lighting and Cooling on a touch panel remember theirs per panel, across restarts.
- On a PC with an Intel integrated GPU and a dedicated graphics card, the Auto render GPU now uses the dedicated card. Rendering on the Intel GPU kept about one CPU core busy in the background.
- Kingston Fury memory updates its lighting faster.
- Newly supported: Corsair K70 RGB PRO Optical, Corsair M75 Wireless on its Slipstream receiver, Corsair Scimitar Elite RGB, Roccat Vulcan II Mini, SteelSeries Rival 3 Wireless, NZXT F280 RGB Core, Razer mice on the Mouse Dock Pro, and the Gigabyte GeForce RTX 4070 Ti SUPER Eagle OC ICE, RTX 4070 Ti SUPER AORUS MASTER, RTX 5060 Ti Aero OC 16G, Radeon RX 9060 XT GAMING ICE, RX 9070 GRE GAMING OC and RX 6750 XT GAMING OC 12G, MSI GeForce RTX 5060 Ti 16G and RTX 5060 8G GAMING TRIO OC, ASRock Radeon RX 7900 XTX Taichi 24GB OC and Colorful iGame RTX 5080 Ultra W OC.

## Cooling

- Import from Nexus 2 (Settings > Privacy & Data) now brings over your fan curves. They apply only to the fans listed, and every other fan keeps its settings. Curves for Q-series and NP50 coolers are skipped for now and show "Not supported yet for this device".
- If Nexus 2 ran in Recommended mode, the import switches Nexus 3 to the matching Silent, Balanced or Turbo preset. If you have already set up cooling in Nexus 3, your setup is kept.
- Importing from FanControl (Import... in the preset menu) changes only the fans it brings over and leaves your preset and every other fan as they were.
- The first time you open advanced cooling, a one-time hint points at Calibrate, so your curves match each fan's real speed.
- Fans pin instead of lock, and hovering a preset tab outlines the fans that preset would change.

## Panels

- On the Y70's page, a new Always on top switch under Show Panel lets other windows appear over the panel, so a window you drag onto it stays in view (with Keep panel clear of other windows off). It is on by default, and Keep the mouse off the panel now sits right under Keep panel clear of other windows.
- On Windows, the 2023 NZXT Kraken Elite and the Kraken Z3 (Z53, Z63, Z73) show a Nexus panel on their screen once Nexus Control is on. The Z3 can instead play a GIF from a file or Klipy.
- The Immersive background option shows only on touch screens, where immersive views exist.

## Deck

- New Deck widgets start with an Essentials set of keys (volume down, volume up, screenshot, Task manager), and Media (previous, play/pause, next, mute) and System (lock, sleep, screen recording, Settings) presets are added next to it, on existing installs too. Your existing decks are not changed. The presets show in your language until you rename them.
- Deck widgets can now use Screenshot and Screen recording keys, which used to be offered only on physical decks such as Stream Deck, plus a new Task manager key. On Windows, Screenshot and Screen recording open the Snipping Tool overlay.
- The Deck widget has a 4x1 size that snaps to single rows.
- Launch app accepts a typed drive path or an http, https or steam:// link, with a Browse button beside the search box and a hint under it when the app is not in the list.

## Nexus 2 import

- The Y70 dock comes over as a 4x1 deck along the bottom of your first panel page, in a preset named Nexus 2 dock: app shortcuts launch the app, files and folders open, websites open their address, one-key recordings become hotkeys, and sound clips play. Importing again updates that preset instead of adding a second deck.
- iFrame widgets become 4x4 Web View widgets showing the same site at the same zoom. A widget with no usable address, or with no room on its page, is skipped with a note.
- The first import turns off Always on top and Keep panel clear of other windows on the Y70, so the panel behaves as it did in Nexus 2. Importing again keeps your choices.

## Widgets

- Weather (4x4): each day's range bar is colored by temperature, with a dot marking today's current temperature. Turn on Accent color bars for bars in your accent color instead.
- Volume mixer: drag devices into the order you want under Output order and Input order in the widget settings, and Nexus remembers it. New Show labels and Show output menu switches, and Hide idle apps becomes Show idle apps.

## Devices

- Asiahorse Umbra V2 support. Each port with LEDs becomes a zone on the Lighting page, or the hub plays its own Sync, Async or Merge effects or follows the motherboard ARGB input. Fans follow the motherboard PWM header or Nexus, and Auto Detect LEDs counts the LEDs on every port. When Nexus lets go, the hub returns to its own effect. Nexus Control is on by default, and the hub's own app counts as a competing app.
- Device rows show when an update is ready: an Update available chip on the Devices page and a glyph in the sidebar, both leading to Firmware Updates. Device warnings become chips with the full message on hover, and a device with Nexus Control off shows neither in the sidebar.
- A device with no icon of its own shows its brand's logo or a generic icon.

## Settings

- With Lighting or Cooling turned off, Nexus no longer takes over your hubs and coolers, and on a new install nothing is taken over until you finish the How much control do you want? step.
- Settings for a feature you have turned off (Lighting, Monitoring events, the thermal guard, drive polling) are replaced by a single note saying which switch to turn on, and search lands on that note. The four feature switches carry a Master switch label.
- With Cooling off, onboarding no longer offers to import fan curves from FanControl or Nexus 2.
- The conflict alert in the top bar shows only when a running app competes for a device Nexus is controlling, and only for a feature you have turned on.
- Drive health (SMART) reads stop while Monitoring and Diagnostics are both off and no fan curve follows a drive.

## Benchmark

- Remove your own run from the leaderboard, from Results or from its leaderboard page. Places past the top 100 show a Top 5% to Top 50% bracket instead of a rank.

## Windows

- When antivirus blocks Setup, its dialog now has a Learn more button that opens the antivirus help page.

## macOS

- With the Nexus app open, the background agent no longer restarts every few seconds.
- Launch app keys accept any app or file path on a Mac, and Browse picks one.

## Phone apps

- In the iOS and Android apps, the panel tray's last tile reads Settings, which is what it opens.

## Fixes

- On some AMD Radeon graphics cards, Nexus restarted every few seconds and lighting and fan control dropped out.
- ARGB lighting could stay on after the PC shut down.
- After the PC woke from sleep, every fan could drop to hardware control.
- In-game overlay settings could stop applying after a window was dragged or a dialog was open.
- Screen Mirror lighting could stay dark when Nexus started with Windows, or after its helper restarted.
- Whenever Nexus connected to its helper, Stream Deck keys sat on placeholder icons and the volume mixer, Deck widgets and Y70 orientation froze for several seconds per app icon.
- Per-app volume, media controls, Deck icons and Stream Deck widgets could stop working after Nexus reconnected to its helper.
- A detected cooler screen with Nexus Control off had no sidebar row, so there was no way to turn it on.
- A Q-series panel that came up late waited three minutes before Nexus restarted it; it now takes about 30 seconds. Repair connection pressed during that restart now runs, and a panel asleep while the PC was locked no longer restarts after the PC wakes.
- The SmartHub page stayed on not connected after turning Nexus Control on, until you left the page.
- Changes on the NP50's page could fail while Nexus was polling the cooler.
- After turning lighting off and on, the Lighting widget could count only some of your devices as lit.
- The static color grid could pick the swatch in the row next to the one you clicked or tapped, and a second finger or palm could steer the pick.
- A value typed into a lighting slider could leave the LEDs on the old value.
- Effect tiles, often the running effect's, could show a broken image until clicked.
- Lighting could freeze on a Corsair Lighting Node or Hydro Platinum cooler that stopped answering.
- The same memory module could be detected twice.
- Weather tiles could show No connection for up to 15 minutes after a restart.
- After an update, a page left open could fail to load; it now reloads once on its own.
- In the Android app, switching between PCs reset both PCs' panel dashboards.
- Nexus could crash when another program held one of its profile files open.
- Panel background files over the size limit are skipped with a message, and picking only unsupported files says so instead of doing nothing.
- A Monitoring chart could crash on an event type it didn't recognize.
