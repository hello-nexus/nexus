## Highlights

- **Lighting comes back on from Advanced mode.** Picking an effect, a mode tab or a color while lighting is Off turns it back on for all your devices. Simple mode no longer takes back devices you turned Nexus Control off for, and a new Control all devices button at the top right of the Lighting page brings every device back when you want it.
- **Lighting uses less CPU on Windows.** Lighting effects now render through Direct3D 11, so on a PC with Intel integrated graphics they no longer keep a CPU core busy in the background. Switching effects is smoother too: the current effect keeps playing while the next one loads.
- **Fan and pump control for the Lian Li HydroShift LCD 360R.** Its fans and pump show up on the Cooling page with live RPM and speed control, and the three fan rings light up per LED on the Lighting page.
- **A Frame cap for each device.** Customize device has a new Frame cap that limits how often that device's lighting updates (Off, or 30, 20, 15, 10, 5 or 1 Hz), for a device or PC that stutters during animated effects.

## Lighting

- Game Sync has been removed due to compatibility issues, and we may bring it back in an improved form in the future. If your lighting was on Game Sync, it switches to Static. On Windows, after upgrading you see the Game Sync tab disabled with this note, and Remove tab takes it away.
- While lighting is Off in Advanced mode, an effect, mode tab or color pick starts that look on every device instead of pinning it to the selected cards.
- Simple mode leaves a device alone when you turned its Nexus Control off: picking a color or an animation, choosing Off, or finishing lighting setup no longer turns it back on. When any device is not controlled, Control all devices appears at the top right of the Lighting page, in Simple and Advanced mode, and turns Nexus Control back on for all of them.
- Frame cap sits beside Save in Customize device, with a tip explaining it. It is off by default, and your choice stays with the device when you switch presets.
- Screen Mirror lighting updates at 30 fps, the same rate as every other effect, so it takes less work from your PC.
- The first time you pin a device, a hint points at its pin: keep the device selected to change its lighting, or unpin it to go back to the lighting for all devices.
- Auto no longer moves lighting to the dedicated graphics card on a PC that also has Intel integrated graphics, since Direct3D 11 rendering removes the CPU cost that change worked around.
- Render GPU in Settings > Lighting & Cooling is now called Rendering device. On Windows it can also pick your CPU, shown by name with "(software rendering)", which frees up the GPU and memory in exchange for higher CPU load.
- On Windows, opening the Mirror tab while another mode is running shows a live preview of the selected monitor.
- On a new install, these mice start with Nexus Control off, because lighting control stops their buttons or DPI from working: Corsair Katar Pro, Katar Pro V2 and Katar Pro XT, Corsair M55 RGB PRO, Corsair M65 RGB Ultra Wired, Corsair Scimitar PRO RGB and HyperX Pulsefire Haste. Turn a mouse's card on to light it anyway. Existing installs keep them as they were.
- Newly supported: MSI MEG X870E GODLIKE MAX and MSI MEG Z790 GODLIKE, including the Z790's MEG logo and the lights below the riser card and along the bottom edge. Thanks @GrPK!

## Lian Li

- The HydroShift LCD 360R gets Fans and Pump on the Cooling page, with RPM and speed control. When Nexus lets go, the pump goes back to motherboard control.
- The HydroShift LCD 360R's three fans light up per LED on the Lighting page as one fan ring.

## Windows

- Effect previews on the Lighting page load faster after your first visit.

## Fixes

- The display on a Lian Li HydroShift LCD with older firmware could stay blank.
- A monitor turned into a panel could lose its layout when Windows gave it a new display id, for example after moving it to another port or reinstalling the graphics driver.
- Lighting and Cooling on a touch panel could drop back to a single selected device after a restart or a device rescan.
- On a touch panel, a palm resting on the static color grid could take the pick from your fingertip.
- When editing a widget on a panel, its settings sheet could cover the widget or sit stranded at the bottom of the screen.
