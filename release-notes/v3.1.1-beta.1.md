## Highlights

- **Production and Beta channels.** Nexus now has a Production channel next to Beta. Change it under Settings > General > Update channel: Nexus shows the version it will install, then downloads and installs it right away, in either direction. Beta keeps getting new features and experimental device support first, and a beta install only ever updates to newer betas.
- **Screens sleep while your PC is locked.** NZXT Kraken, Tryx Panorama, Corsair iCUE LINK LCD, Lian Li SL-LCD fan and HydroShift II screens, and the screens Nexus draws on the PC, now turn off when you lock your PC and fade back in when you unlock it. Each has a Turn off display when PC is locked switch on its page, on by default.
- **Dim lights when idle.** Settings > Lighting & Cooling > Brightness schedule has a new Dim lights when idle option: pick how far to dim and whether it starts after a set time or when your screen turns off. It works whether or not the schedule is on.
- **Corsair iCUE LINK TITAN RX LCD.** The TITAN RX LCD's round screen becomes a Nexus panel, its cap and pump rings play your Lighting page effects, and When Nexus is closed on its page stores an image or GIF on the cooler to show while Nexus is not running.

## Panels

- A monitor turned into a panel from Devices > Displays now gets a grid sized from the monitor's real size, about one row per inch: a 24" monitor gets 12 rows, a 32" 4K monitor 16. Monitors turned into panels before this release move to the new grid, so their widgets may need rearranging.
- A plain monitor panel gets the Widget scale setting: Large shows half as many rows as Small, so widgets stay twice the size.
- The ASUS ROG Ryujin III's screen shows a Nexus panel, fitted to its landscape glass, and the ASUS logo marks it in the sidebar.

## Cooling

- The small fan on the Tryx Panorama's pump head joins the Cooling page as VRM Fan, and the motherboard header the pump runs on is labeled as the pump. When Nexus lets go, the fan returns to the cooler's own setting. Thanks @GrPK!

## Lian Li

- Wireless fans and the HydroShift that lose their pairing with your dongle pair back on their own, as L-Connect does.
- When the wireless dongle stops answering, Nexus resets it in place and keeps the link up, so lighting and fan control no longer drop out while it recovers, and the device list no longer empties.

## Corsair

- iCUE LINK devices carry iCUE's names, such as iCUE LINK LCD Cap and iCUE LINK TITAN 240, and a cooler's screen is named after its cooler.
- When Nexus stops, an iCUE LINK LCD shows your When Nexus is closed choice instead of freezing on the last frame, and a restarted Nexus draws on it again right away.

## Lighting

- The MSI MEG X870E GODLIKE's Dragon, MEG, Enthusiast and GODLIKE logos light up with your effects. Thanks @GrPK!
- The MSI MPG X870E Edge Ti's IO shield joins the Lighting page.
- The AORUS GeForce RTX 5080 MASTER ICE lights its three fans, backplate and side logo as separate zones.

## Widgets

- The Media widget's settings have a Shown app option that keeps one app on screen, playing or paused, and its volume slider follows that app.

## Stream Deck

- A Stream Deck or Corsair Galleon K100 SD that Nexus has never controlled shows on the Devices page with its Nexus Control switch, so you can turn it on.

## Windows

- On Windows 10, the screens Nexus draws on the PC now show the panel instead of staying blank.

## Linux

- On KDE Plasma with Brave or Chrome installed from Flathub, the Y70 shows the same layout as its editor, and changes made on the panel survive a reboot.

## Fixes

- Changing a fan speed or curve while a cooling preset was loaded could freeze Nexus until it was restarted.
- The Mirror lighting effect no longer freezes on its last frame after a fullscreen game starts or the display mode changes.
- Monitor brightness changes made after a pause are no longer dropped, and a short brightness drag on a Y70 Touch no longer leaves brightness unresponsive until Nexus restarts.
- App icons on Deck widgets that stayed blank after a restart now load as soon as Windows is ready.
- The LED dots on the Lighting page go back to their resting look; the live colors trailed moving effects.
