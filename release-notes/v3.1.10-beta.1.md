## Highlights

- **Skins for your panels.** Restyle every widget at once with skins like Pixel, Synthwave, Sakura, Nixie, Blueprint and Sketch, picked from live thumbnails in the panel settings. Each skin remembers its own accent, font and background, and removing it brings your old look back.
- **A sensor watchlist in Diagnostics.** The new Sensors tab tracks any sensor with a 90-day history graph and your own low and high limits. Every CPU, GPU, drive and memory temperature and every fan is listed by default, and each entry can send its alerts to your paired phone.
- **More Lian Li control.** Uni Hub SL V2 gets hardware Merge, Strimer Plus gets every built-in effect with its colors per cable and per port, and HydroShift II radiator fans can be controlled over wireless.
- **See the real color on every LED.** The LED dots in Customize device show the color each LED is being sent, and a note tells you when a brightness setting is dimming them.

## Panels

- Any font works with any skin, and the Pixel font is in the font list.
- Sort the Gallery by name, date modified or date added. The order also applies to Gallery widgets and panel slideshows.
- Wide touch panels, including a HYTE Y70 in landscape, get a wider side drawer.

## Diagnostics

- Pick a sensor on the Sensors tab to see its graph, set its limits on one slider, and choose alerts, urgency and mobile notifications for it.
- Find a watchlist entry straight from the main search.
- Temperature limits now live on each sensor. Limits you never changed move to the new defaults, and the CPU limit starts at your CPU's own maximum temperature.
- Alerts stay on the banner until you dismiss them, and several alerts fold into one expandable banner.
- Critical sensor alerts get through Windows focus mode, and clicking an alert notification opens the Sensors tab.
- Summary, Sensors, drives and fans share one OK, Warning and Critical status.
- Diagnostics notification and health check switches moved to Settings > Monitoring & Diagnostics. Notifications are on by default for new installs.
- Each storage drive has its own health polling interval.

## Lighting

- Color sampling in Customize device picks how each LED blends the pixels it reads: Standard, Vivid or Ignore black.
- Screen Mirror uses less CPU.
- Uni Hub SL V2: Merge runs Meteor, Runway, Tide, Mixing and Stack Multi Color as one animation across all four ports, in the port order you set.
- Strimer Plus: each effect has its own colors and speed, and every cable or port can run its own effect.
- New lighting support: six more MSI RTX 50 cards, Razer Huntsman V3 Pro TKL 8KHz, Razer Chroma Wireless ARGB Controller, Corsair Void Pro headsets, Klipsch ProMedia Lumina speakers, Redragon M901-K-2 and M901P-KS mice, and the ASRock Arc B580 Steel Legend (Linux).
- The MSI RTX 5090 Gaming Trio lights each LED on its own.

## Cooling

- By default only pumps and graphics card fans are pinned. Hub and AIO radiator fans follow your cooling mode.
- HYTE Q60 and Q80: the pump keeps following the cooler's own curve while Nexus drives the fans.
- Lian Li HydroShift II radiator fans show as one Fans row you can control over wireless.
- In the 3D case view, fan labels stay on their fans as you rotate, and a new Labels switch hides them.
- A fan curve whose temperature sensor goes missing shows Not available and holds a safe speed.

## Also in this release

- Thermal Grizzly WireView Pro II readings show under the power supply group. Turn them off with Power monitors in Settings > Monitoring & Diagnostics.
- Monitoring has a Fan sensors category, and a saved sensor that disappears shows Sensor missing.
- Tab bars that don't fit show scroll arrows.
- In narrow windows the sidebar stays compact so pages keep their room.

- Discord and other apps no longer show as muted in Windows when Nexus opens or during a call.
- Mixer presets change only the apps you set a level for.
- Spotify: the full-screen player and widgets keep the right position after a seek or when you open them.
- After sleep, lighting devices come back on their own, and fan and temperature readings no longer freeze.
- RAM lighting stays off through sleep instead of turning back on at the lock screen.
- NZXT Kraken Elite (2023): panels play more smoothly on the screen.
- Corsair iCUE LINK hubs with more than 18 devices list all of them.
- Corsair K100 keyboards light every key.
- MSI and Gigabyte graphics card lighting recovers from a missed update instead of staying on the wrong color or stopping.
- The Razer Chroma Wireless ARGB Controller is found again after you replug its dongle.
- Newer Tryx Panorama SE and Panorama WaterBlock units are recognized.
- A Lian Li HydroShift II connected over USB no longer asks to be bound.
- The Lighting page stays on the effect tab you opened when you come back to it.
- The Swatches grid includes pure red and the other primary colors.
- Sorting the Gallery by name keeps subfolders together.
- Videos in Gallery widgets and Deck keys no longer keep the screensaver and display sleep from starting.
- Unplugging a Corsair Xeneon Edge updates the sidebar and its device page right away.
- The Marketplace's Featured label stays readable over every app's colors.
- Parts you pick on the Build page are saved to your PC setup.
- Linux: the Memory and System health cards show a real status instead of Unknown.
- Linux: the HyperX QuadCast S and other USB lighting devices are detected without extra packages, and the installer names the package to add if one is missing.
- Windows: videos on device screens no longer look washed out on PCs with an HDR display.
- Windows: the dashboard window always opens within your screen.
