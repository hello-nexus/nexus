## Highlights

- **Widget playlists for single-widget screens.** HYTE Q60, NZXT Kraken and other cooler LCD screens can rotate through several widgets. Switch the device's Widgets tab to Playlist, click widgets to turn them on or off, drag to set the order, and choose the interval or shuffle. Arrows under the preview jump to the previous or next widget.
- **Live media backgrounds.** Turn on Live background in the media widget to play a music-reactive effect behind it in the colours of the current album art, from 2x2 up to a new 4x4 size. Two new effects, Lava Haze and Lava Ribbons, also appear in Animate on the Lighting page.
- **Animated weather sky.** Turn on Animated sky in a weather widget's settings to show the current conditions as a moving sky behind it. Clouds, rain, snow, fog, the sun and the moon follow the live forecast and the location's time of day.
- **Pick what Music Reactive listens to (Windows).** Choose the sound output in Settings > Lighting & Cooling or from the Music Reactive button on the Lighting page. Lighting keeps reacting when you change your default output or unplug and replug it.

## Dashboard and panels

- New Auto-arrange switch at the right end of the dashboard toolbar packs your widgets to fit the window and keeps their sizes. It is off by default. The manual dashboard grid is now 16 columns wide.
- New Blank widget for single-widget screens, so a background GIF shows with nothing in front of it.
- Deck widget keys are always square. A custom key image opens a cropper instead of being cropped silently, and a transparent image sits on black on Stream Deck keys instead of a grey tile.
- The Diagnostics widget shows the same four areas as the Diagnostics page: Storage, Memory, Cooling and System. At 4x2, each area opens its own tab.
- Names under volume, mixer and brightness sliders wrap to two lines instead of being cut off.

## Lighting and monitoring

- A clock icon beside the master brightness slider opens the brightness schedule.
- FPS readings can be coloured by value, with low frame rates in the warm colours. A new Reverse scale toggle flips the colours on any value-coloured reading.
- The benchmark's Hardware card shows installed RAM and the peak CPU clock.

## Marketplace

- Each app's page lists its permissions: hardware control, network access, sensors, media and lighting.
- Nexus checks every app download against the Marketplace's signature and removes an app version the Marketplace has withdrawn.

## Also in this release

- The sign-in dialog has a title bar and a close button.
- Settings search finds more settings, including Restart onboarding, smart polling, Discord status, conflicting-app options and Account settings.

## Fixes

- HYTE Keeb TKL: recording a macro or assigning a key no longer fails and wipes the keyboard's key assignments.
- Dragging a device over a collapsed group on the Lighting or Cooling page no longer crashes the page.
- Lian Li wireless: animated effects no longer glitch for a moment as the next part of the animation loads.
- Cooler LCDs such as the Lian Li Galahad II LCD and HydroShift show once on the Devices page instead of twice.
- Panels set to follow Windows light or dark mode switch even while the Nexus window is closed, and desktop widgets recolour right away.
- The benchmark's storage result names the Windows drive it actually tested, not the first disk it found.
- The 1x1 weather tile no longer cuts off its icon.
- Opening the media visualizer no longer switches it to a different effect.
- Reloading a Marketplace app's page shows the app instead of an error page.
