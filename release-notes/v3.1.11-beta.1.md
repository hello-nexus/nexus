This is a quality-of-life release: tons of bug fixes and optimizations across devices from Lian Li, MSI, Corsair, NZXT, HYTE, ASUS, Gigabyte, ASRock, Tryx, VGN and Thermal Grizzly, plus a few new things to try.

## Highlights

- **Your calendar on your panels.** Add Google Calendar or any other calendar by its iCal address and see your events in the Calendar widget, in its immersive Day, Week, Month and List views, and on the new Calendar page.
- **Corsair PSU fan control.** Corsair HXi and RMi power supply fans run on a fixed speed or a fan curve, and PSU temperatures can drive any fan curve (Windows).
- **Smooth GIFs and videos on Lian Li fan screens.** Clips play at their own speed, videos import at up to 60 fps, and each screen has its own Frame rate setting. Thanks @GrPK!

## Panels

- ASUS ROG Ryujin III Extreme: choose a picture or GIF for the cooler's screen from its device page, or keep the hardware monitor.
- Monitoring widgets that list several sensors have a Vertical Bars design: each sensor is a vertical bar with its value underneath, side by side.
- Calendars pick their name from the feed when you leave it blank, get their own color, and link web addresses in event notes. New Calendar widgets start as a date card.
- Add, edit and remove calendars from the Nexus app on your PC; device screens show a note saying so.
- Current readings scale to their recent peak, and rows in one widget share that scale so they stay comparable.
- Marketplace apps on your panels pick up settings changes right away, without a reload.
- Touch monitors used as a panel: long-press on empty space opens the panel menu again.

## Cooling

- Silent and Balanced react more gently to short temperature spikes, with a response time of 5 s and 3 s. Curves you edited keep their own, and any curve's response time can now go up to 10 s.
- Right-click an unresponsive fan to hide every unresponsive fan; the eye button brings them back.
- In the Airflow view, the power supply's fan can be linked to a real fan, such as a Corsair PSU fan. PSU temperatures are listed once, under the power supply.

## Lighting

- Lian Li Galahad II LCD: pick Rainbow Morph, Breathing, Runway or Meteor for the pump ring on its device page.
- Corsair Galleon 100 SD: Save colors to the keyboard keeps your static colors on the keyboard while the Fn and Copilot keys keep working.
- Stream Deck profiles import onto the Corsair Galleon 100 SD, dials included, and the import report lists what came over and what didn't.
- Lian Li Strimer Plus: several controllers run at the same time.
- A device you selected on the Lighting page stays selected if it disconnects for a moment.

## Diagnostics

- Add a drive's data written in the last hour or day, and its transfer rates, to the Sensors watchlist and get alerts on them.
- A drive that stops answering shows Drive not responding instead of an old Good status.
- Drive sensors in the sensor picker name their drive, and drives of the same model are told apart.
- SMART health reads from more USB drives, and RAM sticks show in their own slots with their maker on boards that name slots alike.

## Added support

- VGN Neon 75 HE keyboard lighting; its front strip follows the key color.
- MSI RTX 5070 Ti Gaming Trio: per-LED lighting.
- Thermal Grizzly WireView Pro II Noctua Edition readings.

## Fixes

- A drive that stops responding no longer freezes fan curves and sensor readings.
- Sensor readings no longer freeze while another app reads your RAM; RAM temperatures come back once reads are fast again.
- After sleep, motherboard fans go back to your fan curves.
- HYTE NP50: fans follow your Nexus curves after a cold boot.
- NZXT RGB & Fan Controller: fan readings stay live and the fans no longer restart on a cycle.
- NVMe drives with all their spare capacity no longer show a Warning.
- Lian Li Uni Hub SL V2: every port lights up after a PC restart, and brightness changes reach every port.
- Lian Li wireless fans no longer flash black when a chain joins or the lighting restarts, and fewer chains drop out on large setups.
- Lian Li wireless fan groups no longer restart and flicker when the receiver is busy.
- HYTE Y70 Touch: the screen comes up at your brightness after boot and gets it back when game mode turns the screen on again.
- Monitor brightness changes no longer get lost or land out of order.
- Lian Li fan screens: a GIF or video that can't be imported shows why.
- Lian Li HydroShift II LCD-C on a wireless dongle no longer stops responding after boot.
- MSI motherboards without per-LED lighting: each ARGB header lights its own LEDs instead of the next header's. Thanks @GrPK!
- MSI graphics cards without per-LED lighting keep up with fast effects like breathing and color cycles. Thanks @GrPK!
- Gigabyte RGB memory turns off or goes to a steady color every time instead of keeping the previous effect.
- ASUS ROG Strix SLC IV LCD: the screen comes on faster after Nexus starts.
- ASRock X870E Nova: the board's own lighting maps every LED to the right spot.
- Devices with products picked on their ARGB ports keep their own name in device lists.
- Tryx Panorama: slideshows play videos already stored on the screen to the end.
- Custom sensor labels in widgets show as you typed them instead of all caps.
- Global hotkeys recorded with the keypad Enter or = key work.
- Windows: device screens that show a Nexus panel work again on WebView2 158, which left them black, and recover on their own when they go black or freeze, for example after a monitor change. Shaders and animations on them keep running on the GPU.
- Windows: Nexus starts fully on PCs signed in with a work or school (Microsoft Entra ID) account.
