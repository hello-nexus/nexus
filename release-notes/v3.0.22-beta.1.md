## Highlights

- **Build comes to the app.** Plan an upgrade or a whole new system from inside Nexus. Build opens on the System Builder and lives under Show more and in search. The case you pick for this system is remembered and kept in step with your account on the website. A game's page in Frames has a Find upgrades button, and a benchmark result has Open in builder.
- **Lock a colour on single LEDs.** In a device's LED map, select LEDs and pick a colour for them. They keep it over every effect until you clear them, and the device card, its colour strip and the canvas show which LEDs hold a colour.
- **Breathing with two colours.** Breathing now takes turns between two colours, each set on its own wheel as one colour or a hue range to step through. Sharpness replaces Spread and sets how quickly it switches, so a single colour no longer turns into a rainbow across the device. A black second colour gives the classic breath, and new presets add red and blue, greens over deep teal, and blue, purple and pink with white between them.
- **Upgraded OpenRGB.** Nexus ships a newer OpenRGB and talks to it over its latest protocol. When OpenRGB restarts, devices come back in a second or two instead of up to 11. A replugged mouse or keyboard lights up straight away, and lighting no longer freezes on some devices until OpenRGB restarts itself. The upgrade also adds lighting support for more MSI, Gigabyte, PNY, ASUS and ASRock devices and Kingston Fury DDR5 memory.

## Lighting

- Breathing settings you changed before this update start from the new defaults.
- Speed sits right under the colour wheel on every effect.
- When the colour wheel narrows to one colour, its handle is filled with that colour instead of hiding it.
- In the LED map, the zone tools sit beside the Devices header, LED dots are grey, and a box selection keeps going when you drag past the edge.

## Devices

- Lian Li HydroShift II wireless AIOs on the L-Wireless dongle: pump speed in Cooling (locked by default, like every pump), coolant temperature as a sensor, and lighting for the pump head and the fans wired to it. While Nexus drives the pump, the AIO's screen shows your CPU and GPU temperatures.
- Tryx screens can show GIFs from the built-in GIF search. An upload stays on screen until it finishes, the new media is selected right away, and the preview plays it. Presets now sit below your own media.
- After you turn on Nexus Control, a device page shows Detecting device... until the device is found, instead of a "not connected" message.

## Panels

- A new Immersive background switch: turn it off to see your panel background through fullscreen views. The Backdrop setting is now called Rendering.
- Fullscreen views keep text and controls clear of the screen edge. Touch panels show a one-time hint for closing them with a swipe down, and the swipe works from more places, including the media visualizer and a finger sliding in from above the screen.
- On touch panels, a swipe up from the bottom bezel opens the actions tray every time.
- Adaptive text colour now also covers split-flap tiles and LED and dot-matrix digits, so they stay readable on any background.
- Media widget: a Show volume slider switch, and a Show output label switch (off by default) that names what the slider controls. At 4x2 the playback controls are centred.
- Media visualizer: tap its left or right side to switch effect.
- The Timer widget rings when the countdown ends, and its time blinks until you press Reset. The alarm is on by default with the Beeper sound for 30 seconds. In its settings you can turn the sound off, pick one of ten sounds (each plays when you pick it) and ring for 5, 10, 30 or 60 seconds.

## Account

- My Systems lists the system you are on first, marked This System, with a Sync to account switch. Turn it off to take this system off your account.
- The sign-in dialog has one title and a single line of text.

## Also in this release

- Support bundles include live fan readings.

## Fixes

- Installing an update could sign you out of your account.
- Videos uploaded to a Tryx screen disappeared once Kanali started.
- A Lian Li HydroShift II showed four extra wireless fan ports in Cooling, and fan settings on them could reach the pump.
- Some RGB devices stayed dark after another device was added or removed.
- Lighting settings for some devices, such as an NVIDIA Founders Edition card, could move to another device when RGB devices were added or removed.
- A near-black accent colour picked up from the desktop disappeared in dark theme, and a grey accent turned sliders and icons red.
- A tooltip could stay open on top of a dialog.
