## Highlights

- **One brightness schedule for every screen.** Device screens and monitors can now follow the same brightness schedule as your lighting. Turn it on from the sun next to each brightness slider.
- **Logitech G560 support.** Set the equalizer with built-in or your own presets, turn on surround sound, set each speaker's volume with a test sound, and adjust the subwoofer level (Windows). Thanks @GrPK!
- **Turn device screens on their side.** The new Orientation setting turns many device screens to 90°, 180° or 270°, and a screen turned to portrait keeps its own layout.
- **Your fans' real numbers in Airflow.** Fans you pick in Customize parts show their model, max speed, rated airflow and static pressure, plus the airflow each one moves right now.

## Cooling

- Unresponsive fans show an unplug icon and a tip on why: the fan may be disconnected or having trouble communicating.
- The Airflow view says it's a simulation, with an About this model tip on what Nexus detected and how Customize parts gets you a closer match.
- Airflow always simulates at full detail and shows Simulating airflow while it settles.

## Panels

- Panels play smoothly on the NZXT Kraken (2023) screen, without a light line across the top.
- Orientation replaces the Flip 180° and Portrait mount switches on a device screen's page, and shows only when the screen can turn.
- On wide device screens, the pull-down lays out its sliders and buttons side by side.
- Monitors that support it get a brightness slider on the Displays page.
- Monitoring widgets have a new Scale numbers to fit option that lets the value grow to fill the widget, and dial and wedge gauges get a cleaner layout.

## Lighting

- The 12V RGB header on the ASUS PRIME H510M-E is now supported.

## Fixes

- Lian Li HydroShift II LCD-S and LCD-C: the cooler's own status overlay no longer stays stuck on the screen after boot or wake, and the ring lighting comes back after sleep.
- Lian Li HydroShift II Curved: a screen switched to desktop mode comes back to your Nexus panel without flickering.
- Lian Li wireless setups with many fan chains keep their lighting smooth instead of slowing to a slideshow.
- Setting a fan back to Auto works every time.
- Unpinning a device in Lighting no longer pins it again on your next effect pick.
- The panel preview on a device screen's page no longer dims with the screen's brightness.
- Blower-style graphics cards push their air out through the rear bracket in the Airflow view.
- PC layouts saved in Customize parts pick up your graphics card's airflow again.
- Gallery folders list photos and videos in filename order, so 2 comes before 10.
- Windows: the screensaver and display sleep start reliably while a device screen shows a still Nexus panel.
