## Highlights

- **CPU thermal guard.** Nexus now watches the CPU temperature against its limit and raises fan speeds as it gets close, whatever your curves say. At the limit, or when the CPU heats up at low load (a sign of lost cooling), every fan runs at 100%, and if fan speed changes stop taking effect Nexus hands the fans back to the BIOS. It is on by default in Settings > Lighting & Cooling, where you can also set the CPU temperature limit when Nexus cannot read it from your CPU.
- **Lian Li HydroShift II LCD-S over USB.** Plug in the AIO's USB cable and its screen becomes a Nexus panel you can fill with widgets, the pump, radiator fans and coolant temperature join the Cooling page, and the pump ring plays your Lighting page effects. The ring can also play its own built-in effects, picked from the panel's Settings tab.
- **Dashboard presets.** Save your dashboard as a preset, like lighting and cooling presets, then switch between your presets, rename them or delete them from the dashboard's preset bar.
- **Sentry.** Lock your PC from the Nexus phone app and get an alert on your phone if anyone touches it while you are away. Turn on alerts from the Sentry card in the phone app's actions tray, and see which phones get alerts in Settings > Privacy & Data. Sentry is available in the Nexus mobile apps from version 3.0.1, coming to the App Store and Google Play.

## Cooling

- Fans that could stop while the CPU is hot show a warning triangle on their card; tap it for the reason. Turn the warnings off in Settings > Lighting & Cooling.
- After the thermal guard trips, fans whose setup could not protect the CPU move to a shared Thermal guard curve. A notice lists what changed, with Keep and Undo.
- Diagnostics shows when the thermal guard tripped and how hot the CPU got.

## Lian Li

- Up to four wired Uni Hubs run at once, each with its own device page, lighting and fan channels.
- On the Uni Hub Lighting tab, a port can play its own animation, the inner and outer rings of SL-Infinity and AL fans can play separate animations, and a merged animation runs through the ports in the order you choose.
- AL, AL v2 and SL v2 hubs get their own effect lists and default colors, and SL v2 and AL v2 ports take up to six fans.
- Every Uni Hub, Strimer Plus, TL fan and wireless chain can play its motherboard ARGB header through the sync cable.
- The device page shows the hub's firmware version, and hubs pick their lighting and fan speeds back up after sleep.
- Wireless chains of RL120, SL-INF Flex, TL Flex, SL V4, P28 V2 and CL V2 fans are named and lit with the right LED count. Each chain shows its firmware version, and a fan chain that follows the motherboard header says when its PWM cable is missing.
- A wireless HydroShift II gets screen settings on the Screens tab: brightness, theme, text colors, and one reading or a cycle through several with how long each stays up. Changes show on the screen right away.
- With its USB cable also plugged in, a Nexus widgets switch on the same tab picks what the HydroShift II screen shows: your Nexus widgets, with Manage panel opening the screen's own page, or the AIO's own theme and readings.
- A wireless HydroShift II LCD-S pump ring is laid out around its square screen on the Lighting page.

## Panels

- Swipe past the last page of a touch panel onto a blank page to start a new one.
- A new Keep the mouse off the panel setting on the HYTE Y70 and on monitor panels such as the Corsair Xeneon Edge stops the mouse pointer from moving onto the panel, so you control it by touch only.
- A panel that appears while Nexus is open, such as a HydroShift II plugged in over USB, shows in the sidebar straight away.

## Dashboard

- The dashboard fits the window: widgets that do not fit move below the rest and return to their place when the window is wide enough. This replaces the Auto-arrange switch.
- A Marketplace button next to Desktop Widgets opens the Marketplace from the dashboard.

## Also in this release

- When anonymous usage data is on in Settings > Privacy & Data, Nexus also sends error reports so crashes can be fixed faster.
- Notices across the app share one look in three levels (info, warning and critical), and color pickers line up on the right in every settings list.
- Supported Devices goes back to search and the device table, with each device's capabilities as chips.
- The CPU temperature history is named after your CPU model.

## Fixes

- On a HYTE Y70 Touch with several monitors, the mouse pointer no longer gets stuck on the panel after the monitors sleep or when the mouse moves during a touch.
- Brands with two words, such as Cooler Master, Turtle Beach and Das Keyboard, show their full name in Supported Devices.
- A wireless Lian Li chain no longer shows up briefly as a phantom device.
