## Highlights

- **A more realistic Airflow view.** Your power supply's fan and each graphics card now move air, and you can select several fans and change them together.
- **Whole numbers in monitoring.** The new Monitoring units setting under Appearance rounds readings in your widgets and on device screens.
- **Start presets fresh.** New preset starts lighting, cooling, dashboard, panel and Deck presets from the defaults, and Duplicate current copies the one you have.

## Cooling

- Side radiators pass air only through their fans, and fans spin in the Airflow view.
- Case pressure shows as a chip that explains what positive, negative or balanced means.
- Changing the temp source of a shared fan curve for some of its fans asks whether to change all of them or give those fans their own curve.

## Lighting

- Plasma has a softer palette, and Bubbles adds fine rising particles.

## Panels

- On device screens with adjustable brightness, the pull-down brightness slider dims the screen itself.
- Device screens always show panels at full resolution.

## Also in this release

- Web View widgets can use your microphone and camera when you turn on Allow microphone and camera for that widget (Windows).
- Dialogs fit on phone-size screens, with Cancel on the left and the main action on the right.

## Fixes

- Fans that don't report their speed after calibration show as Unresponsive and stay controllable, instead of Disconnected.
- Corsair iCUE LINK hubs no longer wrongly report another program in control.
- Keyboards that store lighting in onboard memory no longer blink every second.
- Lighting widget color and animation taps work when some devices are pinned, and the newest tap always wins.
- Animation picks in the immersive Lighting view show up in your recent picks.
- Removing a widget while it's open in immersive mode no longer leaves the panel stuck.
- Holding a Deck key opens its menu without pressing the key.
- The widget menu's frosted background fits the menu again.
- Dialogs opened from a touch panel's sheets show at the right size.
- Swipe hints on touch panels show every 30 seconds instead of every 10.
- macOS: voltage, current and power readings show the same decimals as on Windows.
- Windows: the screensaver and display sleep start again while a device screen shows a Nexus panel.
- Windows: dashboard exports and support bundles always download, and the download list opens on the dashboard instead of a panel.
