## Highlights

- **Turn device screens on their side.** The new Orientation setting turns many device screens to 90°, 180° or 270°, and a screen in portrait keeps its own layout.
- **One brightness schedule for every screen.** Device screens and monitors can now follow the same brightness schedule as your lighting. Turn it on from the sun next to each brightness slider.
- **Logitech G560 support.** Set the equalizer with built-in or your own presets, turn on surround sound, set each speaker's volume with a test sound, and adjust the subwoofer level (Windows). Thanks @GrPK!
- **A more realistic Airflow view.** Your power supply, your graphics cards and the real specs of the fans you pick in Customize parts all shape the airflow, and you can change several fans at once.

## Cooling

- Fans you pick in Customize parts show their model, max speed, rated airflow and static pressure, plus the airflow each one moves right now.
- Side radiators pass air only through their fans, blower-style graphics cards exhaust through the rear bracket, and fans spin in the Airflow view.
- Case pressure shows as a chip that explains what positive, negative or balanced means.
- Changing the temp source of a shared fan curve for some of its fans asks whether to change all of them or give those fans their own curve.
- Unresponsive fans show an unplug icon and a tip on why, and stay controllable.

## Lighting

- Plasma has a softer palette, and Bubbles adds fine rising particles.
- The 12V RGB header on the ASUS PRIME H510M-E is now supported.

## Panels

- Panels play smoothly on the NZXT Kraken (2023) screen, without a light line across the top.
- On device screens with adjustable brightness, the pull-down brightness slider dims the screen itself.
- Device screens always show panels at full resolution, and wide screens lay out the pull-down side by side.
- Monitors that support it get a brightness slider on the Displays page.
- The new Monitoring units setting under Appearance rounds readings to whole numbers in your widgets and on device screens.
- Monitoring widgets have a new Scale numbers to fit option that lets the value grow to fill the widget, and dial and wedge gauges get a cleaner layout.

## Also in this release

- New preset starts lighting, cooling, dashboard, panel and Deck presets from the defaults, and Duplicate current copies the one you have.
- Web View widgets can use your microphone and camera when you turn on Allow microphone and camera for that widget (Windows).
- Dialogs fit on phone-size screens, with Cancel on the left and the main action on the right.

## Fixes

- Corsair iCUE LINK hubs no longer wrongly report another program in control.
- Keyboards that store lighting in onboard memory no longer blink every second.
- Lian Li HydroShift II LCD-S and LCD-C: the cooler's status overlay no longer sticks after boot or wake, and the ring lighting comes back after sleep.
- Lian Li HydroShift II Curved: a screen switched to desktop mode comes back to your Nexus panel without flickering.
- Lian Li wireless setups with many fan chains keep their lighting smooth.
- Setting a fan back to Auto works every time.
- Unpinning a device in Lighting no longer pins it again on your next effect pick.
- Lighting widget taps work when some devices are pinned, the newest tap always wins, and immersive Lighting picks show up in your recent picks.
- Removing a widget while it's open in immersive mode no longer leaves the panel stuck.
- Holding a Deck key opens its menu without pressing the key.
- Touch panels: dialogs from sheets show at the right size, swipe hints show less often, and the widget menu's frosted background fits again.
- The panel preview on a device screen's page no longer dims with the screen's brightness.
- Gallery folders list photos and videos in filename order, so 2 comes before 10.
- Windows: the screensaver and display sleep start while a device screen shows a Nexus panel, and dashboard exports and support bundles always download.
- macOS: voltage, current and power readings show the same decimals as on Windows.
