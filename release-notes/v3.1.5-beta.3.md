## Highlights

- **Lighting that reaches sparse LEDs.** Customize device has a new Sampling setting. Each LED can read the area of the effect nearest to it instead of a single point, so effects that pass between sparse LEDs, such as on a keyboard lit only around its edge, are no longer missed.
- **Resize panel widgets from their corners.** Right-click a widget, or press and hold it on a touch screen, and choose Resize, then drag a corner. The widget snaps to the sizes it supports, and the widgets next to it move aside on the same page.
- **Panel looks in profiles.** Settings > Profiles has a new Panels category for the layouts, looks and presets of your panels and cooler displays. It starts out Shared, so every profile shows the same panels until you set it to Per-profile.
- **Sensor reactive panel backgrounds.** The Sensor reactive lighting effects, such as Load bar and Rising heat, can now be a panel's animated background, following your PC's readings live.

## Lighting

- Sampling has four choices. LED reads a single point, as before. Nearest gives each LED the part of the effect closest to it, and Rows and Columns split the effect into horizontal bands or vertical columns, one area per LED. With any of the last three, each LED's area shows as an outline on the Lighting page and in Customize device. Each device keeps its own choice.

## Panels

- On a panel's device page, a widget's resize corners also show when you hover it. Web View and Whiteboard can grow past 4x4 to larger even sizes, up to what the panel holds, and Web View has a new 4x2 size.
- Monitoring widgets have a new Square Ring design, which runs the ring around the tile's rounded corners and also fills wide or tall tiles.
- On a panel's device page, Background mode > Animations has a search box.
- With Panels set to Per-profile, each profile keeps its own panel looks and switches them with the profile. Exported profiles carry them, and importing a profile while Panels is Shared adds its panel looks as presets on matching panels, leaving the look you have in place.
- Panel layouts and cooler display content, including the screens of Lian Li LCD Flex fans, are saved with your profile, so they come back if Nexus starts with its settings reset, for example after a crash.

## Fixes

- On Linux distros based on Ubuntu 24.04, such as Pop!_OS 24.04, 3.1.5-beta.2 kept crashing on start. The Linux build runs on them again.
- On Windows, FPS and Frame Time could show a dash until Nexus was restarted.
- Fit entire image could fail to set a panel background for some picture and screen sizes, for example on a phone.
- On Windows 10, Use as a secondary monitor could not install the display driver it needs, for example on a Lian Li Universal Screen 8.8.
- On a round display, such as an NZXT Kraken's, a monitoring gauge that fills the display, like Ring, showed its value and label too small for the ring.
- A Ring gauge with Gauge colors set drew wider ends than a plain one, and a full one showed a notch where its ends meet.
- In Settings > Profiles, a cloud backup that includes an app not installed on this PC showed the app's internal id instead of its name.
