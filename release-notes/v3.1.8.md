## Highlights

- **See your case's airflow in 3D.** The Cooling page's new Airflow view shows where air flows through your case, where heat builds up, and whether you have positive or negative pressure. Flip a fan between intake and exhaust and see the difference instantly. HYTE Y70, Y50 and X50 for now.
- **Your PC on the dashboard.** The new Build widget shows a picture of your PC, and its larger sizes list your CPU, graphics card and memory.
- **Remastered lighting animations.** Brighter, more colorful effects with presets in color moods like warm, cool, neon, sunset and aurora. Every effect takes its colors from the color wheel.
- **New widget scaling for panels.** Widget scale is a percent slider: go smaller to fit more widgets, or up to 200% on displays with room for it. Existing layouts keep their size.
- **Restricted mode for touch panels.** Lock a touch panel, like a HYTE Y70's, to lighting, media and volume so guests can't change your layout.

## Lighting

- Bounce, Brush and Shine are redesigned, and many more effects are redrawn. Your saved templates still load.
- Simple mode's animations are now Patterns effects you can fine-tune in advanced mode.
- Brightness multiplier keeps your devices' relative brightness as you dim.
- A device that stops responding is flagged, with a Fix lighting guide to get it back.
- Unbound Lian Li wireless fans and Strimers show up on the Lighting page, ready to bind.
- MSI MPG X870E CARBON WIFI onboard lights are their own zones.
- New: ASUS ROG Strix Scope NX TKL Moonlight White, ASUS ROG Zephyrus G16 keyboard, Razer Basilisk V4 Pro, MSI Clutch GM11, Kreo Swarm (wired), TUXEDO Stellaris 16 Gen7 AMD keyboard, Framework Desktop, MSI PRO B850-S EVO WIFI and Z890 GAMING PLUS WIFI6E, and graphics cards from Gainward, MSI, Colorful and ASRock. Several Palit RTX 30 cards work again.
- Better lighting on ASRock graphics cards, Cooler Master ARGB Gen2, Akko PC75B Plus and Mountain Everest 60.

## Cooling

- Fans never stop if fan control stalls: they speed up instead, and one click brings your curves back.
- Lian Li wireless fans stay bound after a full power off, and keep spinning when you close Nexus.
- The CPU overheat guard speeds up every fan, including ones left on motherboard control.
- Keep up to 20 custom fan curves.
