## Highlights

- **Color by value for every sensor.** Power, clocks, fan speed, voltage, throughput and disk space now color by how close they are to their limit, not just temperatures and percentages. Pumps and coolant get their own ranges, NVIDIA GPU power scales to your card's power limit, and disk used/free scale to the drive's size.
- **Your Home Assistant dashboards in Nexus.** The Home Assistant page and widget can show any of your Home Assistant dashboards, with tiles for sensors, scenes, scripts, buttons, covers, fans, locks and helpers. The room view gains search and hides unavailable and Home Assistant-hidden entities.
- **Lian Li Uni Hub synced to an ARGB header.** An SL-Infinity hub can hand its fans to a motherboard ARGB header for smooth, full-rate effects: pick the header and turn on Sync with ARGB header on the hub's Lighting tab.
- **Everything comes over from Nexus 2.** The import now brings every HYTE Y70 background and every Q-Series wallpaper you uploaded, not just the one on screen, and a Nexus 2 wallpaper playlist becomes a slideshow with the same interval.

## Monitoring

- Every sensor type has a sensible ceiling, so gauges stop pegging at full and Fixed ranges start from a useful value.
- Free, Available and Life readings color in reverse, so a nearly full disk reads hot.
- Fan sensors named Pump and temperatures named Coolant, Liquid or Water use pump and coolant ranges.

## Home Assistant

- Pick Rooms or any Home Assistant dashboard as the page's source; the dashboard's views become tabs.
- A Home Assistant widget can show one dashboard, or pin a single view of it.
- Locks unlock only from the detail dialog.
- Collapsed rooms are remembered, unassigned entities come last, and tiles are wider.

## Lighting

- Device menus lead with Customize device and color tuning, followed by the on/off toggles, identify and select, then naming and grouping.
- Port controllers that start every port at 0 LEDs (Thermaltake Riing, Quad and Trio, Corsair Lighting Node, the original Lian Li Uni Hub and similar) now appear with a card and an LED count editor per port.

## Lian Li

- The Cooling tab on the wired and wireless device pages lists each fan with its RPM and how the Cooling page drives it, with a Go to Cooling button. Fan speed and curves are set on the Cooling page.
- The wired Uni Hub page gains a Devices tab for the fan count on each port.
- Uni Hub ports are numbered from 1 on the Cooling and Lighting pages, matching the device page. Saved names, curves and layouts carry over.
- Custom effects on the wired SL-Infinity hub play at the rate the hub can show, without stutter or frames jumping back.
- Wireless fans no longer drop lighting updates when several clusters play busy effects.
- When the L-Wireless Controller's receiver stops answering, Nexus recovers it in place: Windows no longer disconnects and reconnects the dongle, and your wireless fans stay listed.

## Vendor apps

- A new switch in Manage conflicting applications, Shut down conflicting apps as they open, closes the apps you enabled in the list whenever they start, not only when Nexus starts.
- If Nexus could not close an app, a notice says so, with End task.
- The owner switch on a conflict card only shows a selection when the app list and your devices agree.

## Nexus 2 import

- The preview lists what each panel group brings over, including how many backgrounds and wallpapers come over and the slideshow interval.
- Backgrounds come over in the order Nexus 2 showed them, newest first.

## Updates

- The update window no longer sticks on "Starting...".
- When the update check fails, Nexus says so instead of "You're on the latest version", and still offers an update it already knew about.
- An install that does not start retries on its own, and Download & install stays available after any failure.

## Linux

- The HYTE Y70 panel opens in Firefox when no Chromium-based browser is installed (as on Bazzite), and lands on the Y70's screen under KDE.

## Also in this release

- The install folder includes the license and third-party notices.
- The HYTE Keeb TKL icon is redrawn.

## Fixes

- Devices you turned on in Nexus stay with Nexus instead of being handed to their vendor app whenever that app's background services start (since 3.0.21-beta.1).
- The purchases list on the Account page says "Not on this system" for an app that is not installed.
